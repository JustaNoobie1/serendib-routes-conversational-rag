# Theme Guide - Honeymoon and Luxury

**Chunk ID:** SR-021-C001

Honeymoon planning priorities: privacy, room quality, scenery, relaxed pacing, meaningful experiences, and fewer hotel changes.

Suggested multi-region structure for longer honeymoon trips:
- about 2 nights cultural or wellness retreat;
- about 2-3 nights hill-country boutique/premium/luxury stay;
- about 3-5 nights premium beach or coastal resort/villa stay.

This is a theme structure, not a fixed trip length. Do not add the nights together and apply them to a traveler who has not supplied a matching duration.

Suitable experiences where supported and available: private guided heritage tour, tea-country experience, scenic viewpoints, spa/wellness, private transfer, curated dinner experiences, and relaxed coastal time.

Avoid overloading the trip, daily hotel changes, unconfirmed "romantic upgrades", and inventing villa, spa, dinner, or transfer availability.
