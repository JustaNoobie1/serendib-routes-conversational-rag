# Destinations - Hill Country

**Chunk ID:** SR-014-C001

Nuwara Eliya and Ella are hill-country destinations.

Best for scenic landscapes, tea-country experiences, cooler highland atmosphere, viewpoints and relaxed stays, and couples/honeymoon travelers seeking scenery.

Ella is inland hill country. It must never be described as a beach or coastal resort destination.

Rail can be a scenic option on suitable routes, but schedule and seat availability require current verification.
