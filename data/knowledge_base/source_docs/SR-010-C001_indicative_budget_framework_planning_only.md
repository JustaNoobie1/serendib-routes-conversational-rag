# Indicative Budget Framework - Planning Only

**Chunk ID:** SR-010-C001

These are planning examples, not live prices or final quotations.

Illustrative land-plan examples for TWO travelers:
- 5-night comfort-style plan: approximately USD 1,000-1,700 total before supplier quotes.
- 7-night / 8-day comfort-style plan: approximately USD 1,400-2,300 total.
- 7-night / 8-day premium-style plan: approximately USD 2,700-4,500 total.
- 7-night / 8-day luxury-style plan: may exceed approximately USD 5,000-7,000 total depending on properties, private experiences, season, and supplier quotes.

Important:
- These figures are duration-specific examples.
- Do not multiply them into other durations.
- Do not convert them into per-night or per-person rates unless the knowledge base explicitly provides that calculation.
- International flights are not assumed to be included.
- Final pricing requires current supplier quotes and confirmed inclusions.
