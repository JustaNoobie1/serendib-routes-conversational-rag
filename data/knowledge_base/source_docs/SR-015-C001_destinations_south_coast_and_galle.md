# Destinations - South Coast and Galle

**Chunk ID:** SR-015-C001

The south coast includes coastal areas such as Galle, Weligama, Mirissa, and nearby beach regions.

Best for beach relaxation, coastal scenery, Galle heritage and architecture, a slower final stage after inland travel, and couples/honeymoon travelers who want relaxed coastal time.

Galle and Weligama are south-coast choices. Ella and Nuwara Eliya are not coastal choices.

Any marine activity, wildlife encounter, or water-based excursion must be treated as season- and operator-dependent unless current conditions are verified.
