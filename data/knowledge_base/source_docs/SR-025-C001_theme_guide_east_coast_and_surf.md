# Theme Guide - East Coast and Surf

**Chunk ID:** SR-025-C001

East-coast planning can focus on Trincomalee, Pasikuda, or Arugam Bay depending on traveler interests.

Trincomalee: beach, marine scenery, and regional heritage.
Pasikuda: slower resort-oriented beach stay.
Arugam Bay: surf culture and laid-back coastal travel.

Actual surf, sea, and weather conditions are seasonal and must be checked for travel dates.
