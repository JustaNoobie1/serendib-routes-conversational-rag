# Sample Itinerary - 6 Nights / 7 Days

**Chunk ID:** SR-020-C001

This is a TRUE 7-DAY itinerary: 6 nights / 7 days.

Balanced first journey:

Day 1: Airport -> Sigiriya/Dambulla.
Day 2: Cultural Triangle exploration in the Sigiriya/Dambulla area.
Day 3: Cultural Triangle -> Kandy.
Day 4: Kandy -> hill country.
Day 5: Hill-country exploration.
Day 6: Hill country -> Galle / south coast.
Day 7: South-coast morning or Galle, then departure routing
if flight timing permits.

Important regional distinction:
- Sigiriya/Dambulla = Cultural Triangle.
- Kandy = separate cultural/highland destination.
- Ella/Nuwara Eliya = hill country.
- Galle/south coast = coastal region.
Do not merge these regions.

Planning note: a 6-night / 7-day trip is compact. Keep the route focused and avoid adding too many bases.

Honeymoon adaptation: prioritize fewer hotel changes, scenic stays, and relaxed experiences. If transfer pressure becomes too high, remove one region rather than forcing the full route.
