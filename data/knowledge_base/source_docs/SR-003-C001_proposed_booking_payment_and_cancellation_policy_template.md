# Proposed Booking, Payment and Cancellation Policy Template

**Chunk ID:** SR-003-C001

STATUS: PROPOSED TEMPLATE. OWNER APPROVAL REQUIRED BEFORE PRODUCTION USE.

Quotations:
- A quotation is an estimate until Serendib Routes issues written booking confirmation.
- Suggested quotation validity: 7 calendar days unless otherwise stated.
- Prices may change because of supplier rates, taxes, exchange rates, seasonal supplements, or availability.

Payment and cancellation:
- Any deposit schedule, cancellation fee, refund condition, or deadline in this template must be approved by the business owner before being treated as an official policy.
- If the approved policy is not available in the knowledge base, the assistant must say that the current policy needs human confirmation.

Never present this proposed template as an already-approved customer policy.
