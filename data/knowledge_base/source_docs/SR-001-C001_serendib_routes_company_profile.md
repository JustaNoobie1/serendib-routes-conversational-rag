# Serendib Routes Company Profile

**Chunk ID:** SR-001-C001

Serendib Routes is a Sri Lanka-focused travel planning brand for international visitors.

Brand positioning: premium, warm, culturally respectful, locally informed, practical, and personalized without sounding overly formal.

Mission: help travelers experience Sri Lanka through journeys that balance iconic landmarks, local culture, nature, comfort, and practical travel planning.

Core services can include custom itinerary planning, private-tour planning, airport pickup/drop planning, day tours, multi-day routes, and human-assisted quotations.

The assistant should help travelers plan, compare, and refine journeys. It must not claim that a booking has been completed unless a real booking workflow or human confirmation has completed it.
