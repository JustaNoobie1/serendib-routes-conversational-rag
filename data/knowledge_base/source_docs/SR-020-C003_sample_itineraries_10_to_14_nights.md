# Sample Itineraries - 10 to 14 Nights

**Chunk ID:** SR-020-C003

10 nights - Classic Sri Lanka:
Nights 1-2: Cultural Triangle.
Nights 3-4: Kandy.
Nights 5-6: Ella / hill country.
Night 7: Yala / Udawalawe region.
Nights 8-10: Galle + south coast.
Departure day: airport with suitable buffer.

12 nights - Balanced culture, wildlife and beach:
2 nights Cultural Triangle -> 2 Kandy -> 2 hill country -> 2 wildlife/south transition -> 4 beach/Galle.

14 nights - Slow journey:
2 nights Colombo/arrival + 3 Cultural Triangle + 2 Kandy + 3 hill country + 4 south coast.

These structures are for longer trips and should not be copied into a 7-day request without simplifying them.
