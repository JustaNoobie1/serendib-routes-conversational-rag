# Serendib Routes Knowledge Base - Usage and Grounding Rules

**Chunk ID:** SR-000-C001

Purpose: stable planning knowledge for the Serendib Routes travel assistant.

Use this knowledge base for destination themes, route design, itinerary structure, accommodation references, planning principles, indicative planning budgets, and Serendib Routes policies.

Critical boundaries:
- This knowledge base is NOT live inventory.
- It does NOT confirm hotel operating status, tourism licences, room availability, room types, current rates, transport schedules, attraction opening hours, weather, visa/ETA rules, or booking status.
- Dynamic information must be checked from a current official source or confirmed by a human operator before being presented as current or confirmed.
- If a source says "verify", "check", "proposed", "indicative", or "not live", never convert that into "verified", "confirmed", "licensed", "available", or "guaranteed".
- Do not invent missing trip duration, budget, dates, destinations, hotel category, activities, or number of travelers.
- Distinguish days from nights. A 7-day trip is not automatically a 7-night trip.
- Do not calculate a total price by multiplying an indicative daily or nightly band unless a matching total is explicitly supplied in a chunk.
