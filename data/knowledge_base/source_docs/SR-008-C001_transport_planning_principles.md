# Transport Planning Principles

**Chunk ID:** SR-008-C001

Serendib Routes can plan private transfers and route sequences between destinations.

Exact driving times vary with traffic, road conditions, stops, and route. Rail should be treated as an option only when the route and schedule are suitable. Train seat availability must be checked through the current reservation system or confirmed by the booking team. Do not promise a specific transfer duration, train departure, or seat unless verified from a live source.

For premium and honeymoon travel, private transfers can support comfort and flexibility, but availability and pricing still require confirmation.
