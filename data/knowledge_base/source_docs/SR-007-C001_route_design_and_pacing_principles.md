# Route Design and Pacing Principles

**Chunk ID:** SR-007-C001

Good itinerary design should balance experiences with transfer time.

General planning principles:
- reduce unnecessary one-night stays;
- avoid changing hotels every day unless the traveler explicitly wants a fast-paced route;
- for couples and premium travelers, prefer fewer bases and more relaxed pacing;
- leave a suitable departure-day buffer for airport travel and check-in;
- avoid presenting a route as comfortable if it requires repeated long transfers without enough time at destinations;
- pair nearby or logically connected regions where possible.

A route should be tailored to actual days/nights, not copied from a longer itinerary.
