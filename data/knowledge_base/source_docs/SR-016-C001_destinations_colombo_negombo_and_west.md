# Destinations - Colombo, Negombo and West

**Chunk ID:** SR-016-C001

Colombo is best for first/last night, food, urban culture, shopping, museums, architecture, and city orientation. Suggested stay: about 1 night for a transit-focused itinerary; 2 nights for travelers specifically interested in urban experiences.

Negombo is best for an airport-adjacent first or last night, coastal rest, and travelers arriving late or departing early.

For short trips, do not add Colombo automatically if it creates unnecessary backtracking.
