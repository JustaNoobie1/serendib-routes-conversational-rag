# Theme Guide - Culture and Heritage

**Chunk ID:** SR-024-C001

Culture-focused travelers can prioritize the Cultural Triangle, Kandy, and Galle depending on duration and route.

Planning priorities: avoid forcing too many heritage stops into a short trip; respect temple and site-specific etiquette; allow time for active sightseeing such as Sigiriya; and use a geographically coherent sequence.

Current opening hours, closures, ticket prices, and special access rules require verification.
