# Traveler Preference Collection

**Chunk ID:** SR-006-C001

For a personalized Sri Lanka journey, collect the constraints that materially affect the route: travel dates; total days and/or nights; number of travelers; traveler type such as couple, honeymoon, family, solo, or friends; approximate budget or comfort level; interests; preferred pace; arrival and departure airport/time where relevant; and destinations the traveler definitely wants or wants to avoid.

If a critical constraint is missing, ask for it instead of inventing it.
