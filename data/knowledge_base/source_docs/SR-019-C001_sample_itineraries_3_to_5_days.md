# Sample Itineraries - 3 to 5 Days

**Chunk ID:** SR-019-C001

3 nights / 4 days - Heritage Express:
Day 1: Airport -> Sigiriya/Dambulla; light evening.
Day 2: Sigiriya and Cultural Triangle.
Day 3: Dambulla -> Kandy; cultural experience.
Day 4: Kandy -> airport/Colombo depending flight.
Best for: very short first trip focused on culture. Avoid adding Ella and the south coast.

4 nights / 5 days - Hills and Culture:
Day 1: Airport -> Kandy.
Day 2: Kandy -> Nuwara Eliya / hill country.
Day 3: Scenic route toward Ella; light sightseeing.
Day 4: Ella area.
Day 5: Departure routing based on flight and final location.
Best for: hill-country priority. Do not force a beach segment into this short duration.

5 nights / 6 days - Culture + Coast:
Use two main themes rather than trying to cover the entire island. A practical structure is Cultural Triangle + Kandy OR Kandy/Ella + south coast, depending on traveler interests.
