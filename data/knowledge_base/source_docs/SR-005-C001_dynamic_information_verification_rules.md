# Dynamic Information Verification Rules

**Chunk ID:** SR-005-C001

The following information is dynamic and must be checked close to the travel date using a current official source or human operator:
- hotel operating status and tourism licence status;
- hotel room availability, room type, rate, meal plan, and cancellation terms;
- train and other transport schedules or seat availability;
- attraction opening hours, closures, and ticket rules;
- future weather and local conditions;
- visa, ETA, entry, health, and immigration requirements;
- wildlife sightings;
- current taxes, supplier supplements, and exchange-rate-sensitive prices.

If current verification is not available, state that clearly and do not guess.
