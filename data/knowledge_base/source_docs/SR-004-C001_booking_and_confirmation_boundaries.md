# Booking and Confirmation Boundaries

**Chunk ID:** SR-004-C001

The chatbot can help prepare a plan, collect preferences, and guide a traveler toward a quotation or human handoff.

The chatbot cannot, by itself, confirm a hotel room, guarantee a train seat, guarantee wildlife sightings, guarantee attraction access, issue a final booking confirmation, guarantee a visa or ETA approval, or guarantee future weather.

A booking becomes confirmed only when the relevant supplier or Serendib Routes booking team issues confirmation through the actual booking process.
