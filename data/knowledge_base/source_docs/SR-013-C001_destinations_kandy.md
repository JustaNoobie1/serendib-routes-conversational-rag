# Destinations - Kandy

**Chunk ID:** SR-013-C001

Kandy is a central highland cultural destination suited to heritage, local culture, gardens, and route transitions between the Cultural Triangle and hill country.

Best for cultural interest, first-time routes, couples who want a cultural stop without an overly fast pace, and connections toward Nuwara Eliya or Ella.

Kandy is not part of the Cultural Triangle and is not a coastal destination.
