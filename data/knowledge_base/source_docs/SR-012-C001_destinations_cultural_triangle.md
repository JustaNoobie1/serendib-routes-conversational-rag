# Destinations - Cultural Triangle

**Chunk ID:** SR-012-C001

Sigiriya: best for first-time visitors, history, archaeology, landscape photography, and active sightseeing. Suggested stay: 1-2 nights in the Sigiriya / Habarana / Dambulla area. Planning note: the Sigiriya climb can be physically demanding and exposed to heat; allow time and flexibility.

Dambulla: useful as part of the Cultural Triangle and a practical pairing with Sigiriya.

Polonnaruwa: useful for travelers who want deeper ancient-history and archaeology content.

The Cultural Triangle is inland. It should not be described as Colombo, Kandy, Ella, or a coastal region.
