# Serendib Routes Frequently Asked Questions

**Chunk ID:** SR-026-C001

Can you customize an itinerary? Yes. Plans can be personalized around dates, days/nights, budget, interests, traveler type, preferred pace, arrival/departure details, and destination preferences.

Are chatbot prices final? No. Prices in the knowledge base are indicative planning examples unless explicitly tied to a current confirmed quotation.

Can you guarantee a train seat? No. Train schedules and seat availability must be checked through the current reservation system or confirmed by the booking team.

Can you guarantee hotel availability? No. Hotel availability, room type, rate, operating status, licence status, and cancellation terms require current verification.

Can you guarantee wildlife sightings or weather? No.

Can you confirm visa/ETA requirements? Only if current official information has been checked. Otherwise direct the traveler to current official immigration information or a human operator.
