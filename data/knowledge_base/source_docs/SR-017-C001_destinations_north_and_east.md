# Destinations - North and East

**Chunk ID:** SR-017-C001

Jaffna: best for northern culture, history, regional cuisine, and travelers seeking a different regional experience. Because of travel distance, a multi-night stay is generally more practical than a rushed stop.

Trincomalee: best for east-coast beaches, marine scenery, and regional heritage.
Pasikuda: best for resort-oriented beach relaxation and travelers seeking a slower east-coast stay.
Arugam Bay: best for surf culture, laid-back coastal travel, and travelers interested in the southeast/east coast.

Seasonal suitability, surf conditions, marine activities, and weather must be checked for actual travel dates.
