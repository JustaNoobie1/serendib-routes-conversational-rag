# Theme Guide - Family Travel

**Chunk ID:** SR-022-C001

Family routes should prioritize manageable transfer times, suitable pacing, flexible activity intensity, and accommodation arrangements that are confirmed for the actual party.

Do not assume children's ages, room configuration, child policies, or attraction suitability. Ask when these details matter.

Useful themes can include culture, wildlife, beaches, and light scenic experiences, but the final route should depend on family interests and ages.
