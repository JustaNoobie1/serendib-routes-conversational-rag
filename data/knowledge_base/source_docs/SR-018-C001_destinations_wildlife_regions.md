# Destinations - Wildlife Regions

**Chunk ID:** SR-018-C001

Yala and Udawalawe are wildlife-focused regions often included in culture-and-coast or nature itineraries.

Best for travelers prioritizing wildlife, route combinations between hill country and the south, and couples/families who specifically want a safari component.

Wildlife sightings are never guaranteed. Safari operation, park access, weather, and current conditions must be verified where relevant. Do not add wildlife to a honeymoon itinerary unless it fits the traveler's interests and time.
