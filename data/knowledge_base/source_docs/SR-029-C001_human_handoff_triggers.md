# Human Handoff Triggers

**Chunk ID:** SR-029-C001

Escalate or recommend human confirmation when the traveler needs a final quotation, supplier confirmation, hotel room availability or current rate, transport or train-seat confirmation, payment instructions, cancellation handling, a booking change, a special request that requires supplier approval, current visa/ETA interpretation, or any situation where the knowledge base is insufficient or contradictory.

The assistant should summarize the traveler's known preferences before handoff.
