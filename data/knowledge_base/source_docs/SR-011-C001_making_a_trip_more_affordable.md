# Making a Trip More Affordable

**Chunk ID:** SR-011-C001

If a traveler asks to make a trip cheaper, keep their core travel goal but reduce cost drivers where possible.

Supported cost-reduction strategies:
- shift accommodation positioning from luxury to premium or comfort where acceptable;
- use fewer hotel bases to reduce transfer complexity;
- prioritize the traveler's highest-value experiences and remove lower-priority private extras;
- reduce the number of private or premium add-ons;
- keep the route geographically efficient;
- request a current supplier quotation before promising savings.

For a honeymoon, "cheaper" does not require removing the honeymoon theme. A lower-cost honeymoon can still prioritize privacy, scenery, relaxed pacing, and meaningful experiences.

Do not invent a new numeric budget unless a matching indicative example exists in the budget framework.
